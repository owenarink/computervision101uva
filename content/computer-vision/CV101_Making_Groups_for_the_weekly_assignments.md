# Making groups for the weekly assignments

- Due Thursday by 23:59
 - Points None

#### Creating groups

The weekly assignments (programming and theory) are made in groups of two. To form a group, make sure to follow the following steps. **Please note that following these steps is crucial, even if you work on your own (see below). Otherwise you will not be able to submit your assignments.  
  
1. Find a partner**

- Your collaboration partner should be in the same group as you.
- Did you find a partner from another group? Find someone from that group to swap places with, and send an email about this (including the names of both people switching groups) to [e.a.veltmeijer@uva.nl](mailto:e.a.veltmeijer@uva.nl).
- Having trouble finding a partner? You can use the [Finding a partner for the weekly assignments](https://canvas.uva.nl/courses/56444/discussion_topics/1095568 "Finding a partner for the weekly assignments") discussion board to find one. Contact your TA if this does not work out. Please note that it is your own responsibility to find a partner on time.
- Working on your own is not allowed, unless explicit permission was given. Permission, which can only be granted in rare cases, should be requested by sending an email to [e.a.veltmeijer@uva.nl](mailto:e.a.veltmeijer@uva.nl).

**2. Register your group**

If you've formed your group, make sure to follow these steps:

- Assign yourselves to one of the **assignment groups**. You can find these at the [People](https://canvas.uva.nl/courses/56444/users "People")-page in Canvas. Make sure to remember the number: this will be your group number on Canvas, ANS, and CodeGrade.
- Create a group in **[ANS](http://ans.uva.nl/),** using the same number (e.g., 'A1'). Make sure that you are both in this group. Note that you should not start a quiz before both of you are in this group, otherwise the group members can no longer be changed.
- Create a group in **CodeGrade**. Do so by going to CodeGrade via the first programming assignment ([Python assignment week 1: Equalization and Interpolation](https://canvas.uva.nl/courses/56444/assignments/667337 "Python assignment week 1: Equalization and Interpolation")) on Canvas. Change the name of your group to include your group number (e.g., 'Groep A1', as the name should be at least three characters long).  
      
    

#### Submitting assignments

Being in the same group (both in ANS and CodeGrade), only one of you needs to submit the assignment.  
  

#### Changing groups

Changing groups is not allowed. If you face any difficulties or have questions about this, please contact your TA.
